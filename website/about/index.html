Local Knowledge. Personal Attention. Unforgettable Experiences.

Me & You Tours Egypt started on Instagram, sharing the places we love. Today we help travellers turn those places into a holiday that fits them.

Who is behind the brand

Me & You Tours Egypt is a small, personal travel service based in Hurghada on the Red Sea. The name says what we believe in: travel works best as a conversation between you and someone who knows the place.

Why we exist

Many travellers arrive with a long list of offers and no idea which one is right. We want every guest to feel they have a trusted local contact who helps them choose well.

Regions we support

Hurghada, El Gouna, Sahl Hasheesh, Makadi and Soma Bay on the Red Sea coast — plus planning help for Luxor, Cairo & Giza and Aswan.

How we work

We advise, plan and coordinate. The activities themselves — boats, tours, transport — are carried out by licensed local operators. Your quote always names the operator, so it is clear who does what.

Which languages are available?

This website is available in English, Dutch, German and Arabic. The guide language for each activity is confirmed in your quote.
