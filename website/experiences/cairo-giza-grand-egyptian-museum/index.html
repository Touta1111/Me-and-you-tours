Giza Pyramids & Grand Egyptian Museum

The last wonder of the ancient world — and Tutankhamun's treasures.

Plan My Trip

See the Pyramids of Giza and the Grand Egyptian Museum, home to the Tutankhamun collection. From Hurghada this means a flight or a long drive, so we help you choose the most comfortable plan: a day trip or one or two nights in Cairo.

Highlights

  • Pyramids of Giza and the Sphinx
  • Grand Egyptian Museum
  • By air or road — we compare for you

Safety
This is a long day with many hours on the road. Bring water, a hat and comfortable shoes; temples ask for modest clothing (shoulders and knees covered). Follow the operator's instructions at all times and tell us about any health conditions, pregnancy or mobility needs in your request.
