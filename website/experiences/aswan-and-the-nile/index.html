Aswan & the Nile

Felucca sails, Nubian colour and the calm of the Nile.

Plan My Trip

Aswan is the gentlest side of Egypt: white felucca sails, granite islands and Nubian villages. It is far from the Red Sea, so we help you plan it as part of a longer trip — including Nile cruise options between Luxor and Aswan.

Highlights

  • Felucca sail on the Nile
  • Nubian village visit
  • Combine with a Nile cruise
