A Day in El Gouna

Lagoons, marina walks and beach-club calm.

Plan My Trip

El Gouna is a lagoon town north of Hurghada. We help you plan a relaxed day: how to get there, where to walk, which beach club suits you and where to have dinner by the water.

Highlights

  • Transport advice or private transfer
  • Beach club and restaurant suggestions
  • Optional lagoon boat ride
