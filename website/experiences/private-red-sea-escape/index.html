Private Red Sea Escape

Leave the crowds behind. Your boat, your pace.

Plan My Trip

Leave the crowds behind and discover the beauty of the Red Sea at your own pace. Tell us what kind of experience you're looking for, and we'll help you explore suitable private options.

Highlights

  • Private boat for your group only
  • Route shaped around your wishes
  • Ideal for couples, families and celebrations

Practical information

Departure
Hurghada — pick-up point confirmed in your quote
Duration (estimate)
Half day or full day — your choice
Children
Very suitable for families; ask for children's life vests
Included
Depends on the boat and package you choose; every inclusion is listed in your quote
Not included
Anything not listed in your quote
Possible extra costs
Tips for crew or guide are optional. Photos or videos sold on board are optional.
Languages
Guide language confirmed per request
Safety
Listen to the crew briefing, wear a life vest if you are not a confident swimmer, use reef-safe sun protection and never touch coral or marine life. Follow the operator's instructions at all times and tell us about any health conditions, pregnancy or mobility needs in your request.
Cancellation
Set by the operator and shown in writing in your quote before you agree. Sea trips can be cancelled by the port authority in strong wind; you then choose another date or follow the refund terms in your quote.
Price
Price on request — every quote states the total, what is included and any extra costs
Operated by
A licensed local operator, named in writing in your quote before you decide

How booking works

  1. RequestTell us your dates, group and wishes — via the trip planner, WhatsApp or Instagram.
  2. We checkWe check real availability with licensed local operators — nothing is promised before that.
  3. Clear quoteYou receive one clear quote: price, inclusions, operator, meeting point and cancellation terms.
  4. You decideOnly after your approval is anything reserved. Payment, if any, goes only through the official method in your quote.
