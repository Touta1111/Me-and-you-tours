Questions

Good to know before you ask.

How do I request an experience?
Open the experience and tap 'Request availability', use the trip planner, or simply message us. You review the message and send it yourself.
Can you help me plan a private trip?
Yes — that is what we enjoy most. The trip planner asks the right questions; we come back with options that fit you.
Are prices confirmed before booking?
Always. You receive a written quote with the total price, inclusions and conditions. Nothing is reserved without your approval.
Who operates the excursions?
Licensed local operators. The operator is named in your quote, so you always know who is responsible on the day.
Can you help with airport transfers?
Yes, on request through licensed transport partners. A ride is only confirmed once the licensed driver or company has accepted it.
What happens if an activity is cancelled?
The cancellation and refund terms are in your quote before you agree. If the operator or authorities cancel (for example a sea trip in strong wind), we help you rebook or follow the refund terms.
