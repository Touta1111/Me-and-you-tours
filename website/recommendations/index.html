Travel guide

Practical, honest tips for your stay on the Red Sea. Independent advice — we will always say so if a recommendation is a paid partnership.

Local tips

Local Recommendations

Money & payments

  • The local currency is the Egyptian pound (EGP). Exchange money only at banks, official exchange offices or bank ATMs, and keep your receipt.
  • Many hotels, restaurants and shops accept cards; small cash is useful for tips and small purchases.
  • Never transfer money to a personal account for 'better exchange rates' or to hold your money.

Local customs

  • Resort wear is fine at the hotel and beach; cover shoulders and knees in towns, mosques and temples.
  • Ask before photographing people. Tipping is common for good service.
  • Friday is the weekly day of prayer; some offices and shops keep shorter hours.

Getting around

  • Agree the taxi fare before you get in, or use a ride-hailing app if it is available at your location.
  • For airport rides and long distances, a pre-arranged licensed transfer is the calmest option.

The Red Sea

  • Coral is alive: don't stand on it or touch it, and use reef-safe sun protection.
  • Wind can change sea conditions quickly; boat trips may be cancelled by the port authority for safety.
  • The sun is strong all year: drink plenty of water and take shade in the middle of the day.

With children

  • Choose shorter boat trips and shaded beaches for young children; ask for children's life vests.
  • Long day trips (Luxor, Cairo) are easier with an overnight stay.

Restaurants & beach clubs

  • Hurghada's Marina and El Gouna's Abu Tig Marina have many restaurants by the water — ask us for suggestions that fit your taste and budget.
  • Try local dishes such as koshari, fresh grilled fish and Egyptian sweets.
