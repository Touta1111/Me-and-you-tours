Practical

  • Currency: Egyptian pound (EGP). Check the day’s rate with the Central Bank of Egypt before exchanging.
  • Sun: strong all year — sunscreen, hats and plenty of water for the children.
  • Reef: don’t touch or stand on coral; use fins or water shoes.
  • Emergency: ambulance 123 · police 122 · tourist police 126.
  • Transport: arrange taxis through your host or agree the price before you get in.
