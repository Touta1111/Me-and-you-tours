Why Hurghada

Sea, desert and history in one stay

Hurghada sits on Egypt's Red Sea coast. Boats leave from the city's marinas to the protected Giftun Islands; the Eastern Desert begins right behind the town; and Luxor's temples can be reached on a long day trip.

  • Island days and snorkelling on the reefs
  • Desert evenings with the Bedouin
  • Day trips to El Gouna, Luxor and Cairo
  • Sunny weather most of the year
