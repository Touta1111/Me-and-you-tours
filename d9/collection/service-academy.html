The problem

Good people, no time to train them, and guests who notice.

i

New every season

New staff start in the busiest weeks. They learn from whoever is on shift — good habits and bad ones.

ii

The words that cost a review

"That's not my problem" takes two seconds to say and stays online for years.

iii

No training manager

Large hotel chains have learning teams. Independent hotels, restaurants and beach clubs usually do not.

The solution

Short, practical lessons built on real moments from the floor.

Every training uses situations your team meets every day: what the guest says, what not to answer, what a professional answers — and why. Staff practise in role plays and short digital lessons, and managers receive a clear report.
