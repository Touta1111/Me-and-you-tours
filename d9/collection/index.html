What we do

Each service, step by step.

01
Hospitality quality audits

Secret Guest

A trained evaluator visits your hotel or restaurant as a normal guest and scores the complete guest experience.

For: Hotels · restaurants · beach clubs · holiday rentals

How it works
  1. Scope and criteria agreed in writing
  2. A discreet visit as a real guest
  3. A second reviewer verifies every finding
  4. Confidential report and debrief with management
You receive

Scores in 9 categories, time-stamped observations, a risk overview and 5 priorities

Price

From EGP 9,500 per audit

No recordings · no staff names in reports

02
Hospitality revenue verification

Revenue Shield

We match every reservation from your booking channels with the payout on your bank statement and flag every difference.

For: Owners of holiday homes · small hotels

How it works
  1. You share read-only exports, never passwords
  2. We match every booking to a payment
  3. Each difference is flagged with its evidence
  4. You receive an overview and a next step per flag
You receive

A clear control overview with flags by category and status

Price

Pilot of 30 bookings: EGP 1,500

Administrative check · not accounting or tax advice

See the full service and demo →
03
Premium luggage storage

Luggage Lounge

A staffed, secure lounge in central Hurghada where travellers leave their bags between check-out and their flight.

For: Travellers · families · cruise and day visitors

How it works
  1. Reserve online or walk in
  2. Bags checked, tagged and sealed with you
  3. A digital receipt with a unique pick-up code
  4. Collect with your code and name
You receive

A digital luggage card and sealed bags in a locked, staffed room

Price

From EGP 120 per bag

Opens only with an approved location and insurance
