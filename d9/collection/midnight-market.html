Three formats

From one elegant shelf to a micro market.

Mini Corner

One cabinet near reception with around 40 essentials. Staff take the payment. Ideal for apartment buildings and small hotels.

Smart Shelf

A styled sales wall with clear prices, a card reader and an InstaPay code. Around 70 products, checked twice a week.

About 2–3 m² · from EGP 60,000 set-up (to be quoted)

A small room with chilled drinks and up to 120 products, for locations with enough guests, safe space and staff.

How it works

A small pilot before any big step.

Visit

We look at your guests, your space and the shops nearby.

Propose

Format, range, prices and the partnership model, in writing.

Pilot

Eight weeks with a small range, so we measure real demand.

Report

Monthly sales, margin, losses and the best-selling products.

Grow

Only when the numbers work do we expand the range or format.
