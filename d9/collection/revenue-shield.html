The problem

Bookings come from five places. Money arrives from three. Nobody matches them line by line.

i

The payout that never came

A three-night stay is in your calendar and your channel manager. The platform payout for it is not on your bank statement.

ii

Commission on a cancelled stay

The guest cancelled in time, but the commission invoice still charges you for the booking.

iii

The rate that changed

You sold the room at one price. The payout is lower, and the difference is never explained.

The solution

We match every reservation with what was actually received.

You share read-only exports from your booking channels, channel manager and bank or payout statements. We reconcile them line by line and flag every difference with the evidence next to it.

  • 1Reservations that do not match a recorded payment
  • 2Commission invoices that need checking
  • 3Cancellations, duplicates and unexplained differences

Revenue Shield is about financial control, not about getting more bookings.

How it works

Read-only data. Clear evidence.

Agree

We sign a service and confidentiality agreement and fix the period and sources.

Share

You send exports or read-only access. We never ask for passwords to payment accounts.

Match

Every reservation is matched with a payout, invoice or bank line.

Flag

Each difference gets a category, the evidence and a status.

Report

You receive the overview and the next step for every flag.

Clear boundaries

An administrative control service — and nothing it is not.

✓
